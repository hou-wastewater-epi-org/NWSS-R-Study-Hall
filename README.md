# Welcome to the NWSS R Study Hall!
 
The NWSS R Study Hall is a collaborative space hosted by the Houston NWSS Center of Excellence for members of the NWSS community to learn, share knowledge, and build skills in R programming with a focus on wastewater surveillance.
 
## Goals

- Provide R support tailored to wastewater surveillance
- Foster collaboration across the NWSS community
 
## Meeting Information

- Frequency: Every other month (bi-monthly)
- When: Third Tuesday of the month
- Time: 2:00 PM Central Time
- Format: Collaborative study hall with presentations
 
## Want to Join?

Contact Rebecca Schneider at rebecca.schneider@houstontx.gov to request an invitation.

Whether you're new to R, looking for help with a specific analysis, or have experience to share, everyone is welcome. Bring your questions, ideas, and curiosity!
